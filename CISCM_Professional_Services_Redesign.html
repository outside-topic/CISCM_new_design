How we work

From the first conversation
to an agreed conclusion.

Seven stages give the engagement structure. Select a stage to see what it is intended to establish.

Discover

Clarify the purpose, intended users, constraints, risks, desired outcomes and decision authority.

Scope

Agree the deliverables, exclusions, responsibilities, milestones, evidence, fees and acceptance criteria.

Design

Confirm the method, expertise, data needs, quality checks, communication and change control.

Deliver

Carry out the agreed work through approved personnel and controlled materials.

Verify

Review quality and acceptance criteria, gather feedback and record unresolved matters.

Close

Issue final outputs, transfer approved materials and confirm record-retention arrangements.

Follow up

Evaluate the agreed outcomes and identify corrective action, renewal or next-stage support.

The written scope confirms the actual responsibilities, evidence, fees, milestones and acceptance arrangements for your engagement.

Scope & context

Evidence & constraints

Ownership & acceptance

Ask CISCM a question →
How do I choose the right service?

Start with the outcome you need and the issue you are trying to address. Compare the service descriptions, then discuss scope and evidence with CISCM before agreeing the work.

Does advisory work guarantee accreditation or certification?

No. Advisory or developmental work does not guarantee accreditation, certification or business results. Recognition depends on the applicable criteria and formal decision.

Can corporate training be customized?

Corporate training solutions are described as being built around needs analysis, role requirements, measurable outcomes, suitable delivery and agreed evaluation.

Does career support guarantee a job or promotion?

No. Career and job-readiness support does not guarantee employment, salary, promotion or immigration outcomes.

What should be agreed before work starts?

The written scope should define deliverables, responsibilities, evidence, milestones, fees and acceptance criteria, together with relevant exclusions and controls.

Where can I confirm fees, timescales and service availability?

Contact CISCM with a short description of your need. Confirm the current service arrangements and written scope before committing to an engagement.
